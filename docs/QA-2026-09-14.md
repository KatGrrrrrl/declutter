# Check-up — Sep 14, 2026

_Plain-language summary first; the technical notes are at the bottom for whoever fixes things._

## The short version

**Signing in works.** We tested it three different ways with throwaway accounts, and every
test passed. Owners can do owner things, helpers can only do helper things, and strangers
get nothing. Typing a wrong password shows a polite message instead of breaking anything.

**The "wrong tabs after refresh" bug is fixed and live.** Last week, refreshing the page as
the owner could drop you into the helper's version of the app. That is gone, on phones and
computers.

**The big rebuild of the app's login and sync core is finished and live.** The tests also
show that deleted items stay deleted, and that something photographed while offline still
arrives later, even if someone else signed in on that phone in between.

## What we did not test

- Signing in with the emailed six-digit code (the test would have sent real emails).
- Signing in with Google (needs a real Google account).

Both buttons are there and wired up; we just didn't press them.

## What still needs attention

- **Payments are still in test mode.** Nobody can be charged until the real Stripe key goes in.
- **A made-up person, "Rebecca, your executor", appears for every real family.** She is
  demo content that leaked into the real app.
- **"Export PDF" says "coming in Phase 2".** The memorandum export isn't built yet.
- **"Cancel anytime" has no cancel button.**
- **Readability fixes for the parent's screens:** some labels are too faint and some
  buttons too small for older eyes and fingers (the list from Sep 9).
- **A harmless but noisy error fires on every page load** (a technical mismatch between
  the pre-built page and the live one). Nothing looks broken; it needs a design decision.
