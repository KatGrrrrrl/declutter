## A. Blockers — fix before anyone real uses it

**A1. Owner reload or deep link lands in the helper app.** Four routes exist in
both tab groups — `account`, `capture`, `family`, `inventory` — at the same URL.
On a direct load expo-router picks `(child)`, and `(child)/_layout.tsx` has no
role guard. Verified live: an owner on `/account` or `/inventory` who refreshes
gets Capture / Rooms / Inventory / Family and no Decide tab. On mobile the
Account tab is the one people refresh on. Fix: role guard in `(child)/_layout`
(redirect owners to `(parent)`), or rename the child routes.

**A2. Fictional executor "Rebecca, Family attorney · Designated" shows for every
real household.** Hard-coded in `(parent)/export.tsx:134` and all of
`(parent)/legacy.tsx`. An elder sees a named attorney they never appointed.
Hide unless demo data is loaded, or show "No one named yet".

**A3. Owner's manual backup fails once a contributor has captured anything.**
`sync.ts:203-209` `itemRow()` always writes `created_by = userId`; the owner's
upsert is `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, and migration 0001 raises on any
`created_by` change. Symptom: "Backup didn't finish: items.created_by is
immutable". Reconcile and `pushItemUpdates` are unaffected.

**A4. One shared `items/people/rooms/members` array serves every household.**
`switchHousehold` and `addHousehold` only change `activeHouseholdId`
(`store.ts:1007-1072`). Open home A, switch to B: B shows A's items, and
CloudBridge reconciles A's items into B's cloud household. Backup of a new B
then upserts A's item ids under B and trips `items_guard`. (Thread 6 handed
back the `addHousehold` half of this.)

**A5. Offline decisions and deletes are silently reverted.** `pushItemChange`
and `removeItem` swallow errors (`store.ts:717-736`, `1427-1438`);
`mergeCloudData` overlays the cloud decision unconditionally on the next
connect. A swipe made without signal is lost with no message; a deleted item
comes back.

**A6. Web photos die on reload.** `pickPhoto()` on web returns a `blob:` URL
that is persisted as `photoUri`; `PhotoBox` prefers `photoUri` over
`remotePhotoPath`, so even a successfully uploaded photo shows as a grey
initial after refresh on the capturing browser. Nothing clears `blob:` URIs.

**A7. Onboarding accepts blank name fields and silently adopts the
placeholders.** Tapping Continue on step 2 with nothing typed creates a
household called "The Lakehouse" owned by "Rose". Verified live.

## B. Accuracy — the app says things that aren't true

**B1. Memorandum export is a stub.** Preview and Export PDF both toast "Coming
in Phase 2". Yet `AGENTS.md:140` says the memorandum export shipped, and the
Welcome page promises "export it when you're ready". Either build it or
correct both docs and the Welcome copy.

**B2. "Cancel anytime" with no cancel path.** `upgrade.tsx:51,67`. Settings
itself admits "A cancel button is on its way — get in touch", with no address.
Put `hello@inventoryourhouse.com` there (already offered) or add a Stripe
customer-portal function.

**B3. Insurance inventory / Donation tax receipts cards are inert** with no
"coming soon" marker (`export.tsx:100-116`).

**B4. "Continue with Apple" is shown on web** in Account & sync; Apple OAuth is
not configured, so it dead-ends with "isn't ready yet". `login.tsx` correctly
omits it.

**B5. Settings says "No password: we send a six-digit code"** while password is
the default on the sign-in page. Same app, two stories.

**B6. Voice recordings store a literal transcript** "(Transcription coming
soon — your voice is saved with this piece.)" in `story.transcript`
(`item/[id].tsx:249`) and render it in quotes as the parent's words; it syncs
and will export as data.

**B7. Privacy row → "Coming soon"** on a live site; GO-LIVE has no privacy
policy line item. Also Settings "Your inventory stays on this device" is static
copy that stays wrong after a backup.

**B8. Empty Decide on a brand-new home reads "All decided. Beautiful."** A
household with zero items has decided nothing.

**B9. "View as helper" from the demo returns to the Welcome page**, not the
helper view (`setRole('contributor')` then `router.replace('/')` with
`onboarded:false`). Verified live.

**B10. "Back to Export" from Legacy lands on Decide** (`router.back()` after a
tab switch). Verified live.

**B11. Stale pricing residue.** Capture's limit-reached copy would print
"You've catalogued Infinity items"; `addHousehold` failure still routes to
Upgrade; `ItemQuotaMeter` renders nothing but is still mounted;
`create-checkout` comment says $49.99/yr. Harmless today, forbidden by the
pricing rule.

**B12. Docs drift.** `HANDOFF.md` repo map says 0001–0012 and 9 functions
(15 and 10); §9 "most recent work" describes the superseded cloud paywall;
`upgrade.tsx` map line says backup paywall; `SPEC.md:370` still says
RevenueCat. `AGENTS.md` sender rule says all mail is `hello@…`, but sign-in
codes and `invite-member` go through Supabase's built-in mailer.

## C. Correctness (static review, verified by reading)

**C1. A device that onboarded while signed in is never bound to the account**
(`bindAccount` only runs from `adoptSnapshot` and inside `reconcileAccount`),
so the first re-login stashes its state under the orphan key and replaces it
with the cloud copy — `localOnly` items vanish from the UI.

**C2. `viewerName` returns `ownerName` for any `role==='owner'`**, so a second
decider's swipes, rooms and collections are stamped with the first decider's
name, and `selectIsAdmin` checks the wrong person. Also hits the original owner
after Restore if the roster comes back in a different order.

**C3. Sign-in race.** CloudBridge reconciles on `SIGNED_IN` with the previous
account's `s.items` before `reconcileAccount` stashes them; if the new account
shares the household, those items are inserted with the wrong immutable
`created_by`.

**C4. `mergeCloudData` clears every local heir when the `heir_assignments`
read returns nothing** (query error → empty map), and never merges `people` or
`members` although the pull returns both — an heir added on the phone has no
name on the laptop.

**C5. Case-only room rename** ("study" → "Study") leaves items in a ghost room
and lets an admin delete a non-empty room.

**C6. Helper keeps a stale "revealed" heir** after the owner flips it back to
private (RLS hides the UPDATE; no delete arrives).

**C7. `plan` is device-global** while `household_plans` is per household — UI
only; the server still gates AI.

**C8. Web photo data-URIs are persisted into `localStorage`** via AsyncStorage;
the ~5 MB quota would make persist writes fail silently. Mechanism visible,
quota behaviour not verified.

## D. Mobile flow (native paths, from code)

**D1. Swipe gesture commit relies on Reanimated's `withTiming` callback**, which
the file itself says is unreliable on web; the button path was moved to a JS
timer, the gesture path was not. Also swipe-down collides with Android
pull-to-refresh (no `overscroll-behavior`) and a Keep drag from the left edge
triggers iOS back-swipe.

**D2. Presence and Restore banners mount outside any SafeAreaView** — under the
notch, with a doubled top inset below.

**D3. Camera "name it" card has no `KeyboardAvoidingView`** and the input
auto-focuses, so Discard and Split with AI sit under the iOS keyboard after
every shot. Same on item detail (chat input, value field).

**D4. Deep link or refresh into `item/[id]`, `collection/[id]`, `settings`,
`upgrade` is a dead end** — their only exit is `router.back()`.

**D5. Login has no back or cancel when not locked out**; "Loading your home…"
has no timeout; a failed household load re-shows the form to an already
signed-in user.

**D6. First launch flashes Welcome** before the persisted store hydrates (no
hydration gate). Same mechanism as the web #418.

**D7. Camera and recorder files stay in the cache directory** — iOS may purge
them; voice stories never sync, so that is silent loss.

**D8. Native capture is camera-only**: no "no photo" and no library picker.

**D9. Hidden-tab screens** (parent capture, heirs, family) have no Back.

**D10. Raw network errors** ("Failed to fetch") reach the user on sign-in.

**D11. `notify()` is `window.alert` on web** — blocking dialogs for "Backed up".

## E. Accessibility

Counts: 150 raw `Pressable`s, 82 without a label (~35 contain an icon glyph and
announce garbage, the rest get a usable name from content); 15 `TextInput`s
with placeholder-only labels; 4 images without alt; 3 controls are `Text
onPress` (mouse-only on web).

**E1. Decide's three action captions** (Let go / Donate / Keep) are 11 px
uppercase in `inkFaint` — 2.79:1, far below AA, on the parent's most important
buttons.

**E2. `inkFaint` (#A09A90) as text** fails AA everywhere it's used for real
text: tile subtitles, timestamps, the export disclaimer, decider flags, and
every placeholder.

**E3. `Btn kind="brass"` label** (white on #A67C34) is 3.78:1 — fails for
normal text. Used for "Add" on Heirs.

**E4. Mouse-only controls**: "Estimate value" in Keepsakes and the inventory
list, and "Different email" on the code-entry step — the only way back if you
mistyped.

**E5. Onboarding step 1**: Back and both role cards are unlabeled; step 2's
inputs are named only by their placeholders; "Skip for now" and "Explore with
sample items" are unlabeled.

**E6. Small targets on parent screens**: Decide's "Add" pill is 33 px tall
(measured), Keepsakes' "Who inherits what" — the only route to Heirs on mobile
— is 37 px.

**E7. Toggles without `accessibilityState`**: room chips, decider chips, the
room-browse toggle, and both two-tap "armed" danger buttons in Settings (a
two-tap confirm is also a fragile pattern for elders).

**E8. Sheets are not dialogs**: `CollectionPicker` has no `role=dialog` /
`aria-modal`, and its backdrop and sheet are two unnamed tab stops.

**E9. Welcome's "Sign in" link** is named "Already set up a home?" — the
actionable word isn't in the name.

What's good: `Btn`, `Title`/`Heading` landmarks, one `main` per screen, the
named `nav`, `DecorativeIcon` in both tab bars, live regions on the login error
and presence banner, core theme contrast (ink 17.7:1, brassDeep 6.0:1), the
64–76 px decision buttons.

## F. Backend advisors

- Three `SECURITY DEFINER` RPCs callable by `authenticated`
  (`accept_invite`, `decline_invite`, `my_pending_invites`) — intentional,
  but review that each checks `auth.uid()` internally.
- Leaked-password protection is off; only one MFA option.
- Unindexed FKs: `heir_assignments.person_id`, `items.is_duplicate_of`.
- Two permissive UPDATE policies on `household_members` (perf only).
- Unused indexes: `items_household_decision_idx`, `items_collection_idx`,
  `heir_assignments_household_idx` — expected on an empty DB.
