How it goes

Three passes through a house, and it's done.

Nobody has to sit in a storage unit with a bin bag and a deadline.

Pass 01

They photograph a room

One tap per object. A child, a neighbour, anyone helping can clear a whole room in an afternoon.

Pass 02

You decide, from your chair

Keep, donate or let go — one object at a time. Your say is final, and you can change your mind.

Pass 03

You say why it matters

Record the story in your own voice, and quietly note who a keepsake should go to. Export it all when you're ready.

What we promise

The tender parts stay yours.

  • PrivateWho gets what is visible only to you, until you choose to reveal it.
  • ProtectedPhotos are stored privately and stripped of location data before they're saved.
  • ReversibleNothing is deleted by deciding. Archive it, undo it, change your mind next week.
  • UnsoldPaid for by subscription. No advertising, and your family's things are never sold as data.
